Who We Are

iQunube (PTY) LTD

100% black-owned South African company and an iNAMBA NATION subsidiary. We are the world's first manufacturer of vehicle-matched automotive accessories — products designed to look like they came from the factory, not from an aftermarket shelf.

All intellectual property is solely owned by founder Thabo Migcobo Namba. Our design studio and headquarters are in Gqeberha, Eastern Cape — the automotive heartland of South Africa.

Our Products

Five Product Categories

Each product is designed, engineered, and colour-matched to a specific vehicle model. Carbon fibre construction. Aerodynamic profiles. Smart technology integration.

Aero-Cargo Pods

Carbon fibre rooftop cargo pods with teardrop aerodynamics, integrated LED light signatures, and solar charging panels.

AeroRack System

Modular carbon fibre roof rack with SnapBrace quick-release mounting and PodLink adapter for seamless pod integration.

Towbar-Mounted Carriers

Vehicle-inspired rear design with integrated LED tail lights that mirror the vehicle's own light signature.

Smart Cooler Box

Vehicle-branded editions with colour-matched trim, embossed model insignia, and Bluetooth temperature control.

Beijing Transit Module

The world's first all-in-one smart transit module. Solar power, biometric security, CCTV, and vehicle jumpstart.

Safari & Adventure

Built for the African terrain. Dust-sealed, shock-resistant, and adventure-proven across every product line.
